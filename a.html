Lamborghini is an Italian luxury car manufacturer known for producing high-performance sports cars. Founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini, the brand has become synonymous with speed, style, and exclusivity. Lamborghini vehicles feature cutting-edge designs, powerful engines, and advanced technologies, making them iconic in the automotive world.


lamborghini
  • Aventador: Known for its V12 engine and futuristic design.
  • Huracán: A V10-powered supercar, more accessible but still powerful.
  • Urus: Lamborghini's luxury SUV, combining supercar performance with off-road capability.
  • Miura (1966-1973): Widely considered the first supercar with a rear mid-engine layout, the Miura revolutionized automotive design and set the standard for future high-performance cars.
  • Countach (1974-1990): Famous for its sharp, wedge-shaped design and scissor doors, the Countach became an icon of 1980s supercar culture.
  • Diablo (1990-2001): The first Lamborghini capable of exceeding 200 mph (320 km/h), it was celebrated for its aggressive design and raw power.
